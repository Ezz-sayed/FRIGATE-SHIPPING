OUR SERVICES

Logistics Solutions Built Around Your Business.

From global freight to final delivery, we provide reliable, secure, and efficient logistics solutions designed to keep your business moving.

01

Ocean Freight

At Frigate Shipping Group, we deliver world-class ocean freight solutions tailored to your specific cargo needs. We specialize strictly in Full Container Load (FCL) shipments, offering a comprehensive range of equipment to accommodate any cargo type.

FCL • Dry • Reefer ↗
02

Air Freight

When time is of the essence, our expedited air freight services ensure your most critical shipments take flight immediately. We partner with top global air carriers to guarantee rapid transit times, maximum security, and prioritized handling for your time-sensitive cargo worldwide.

Fast • Secure • Global ↗
03

Land Transport

Connecting your business beyond the ports. Our comprehensive overland transportation services feature a highly reliable trucking network dedicated to safe, punctual, and flexible regional distribution, cross-border transport, and seamless door-to-door delivery.

Regional • Cross-Border ↗
04

Customs Clearance

Navigating customs regulations can be complex, but our expert team ensures a smooth and hassle-free process. We handle all necessary documentation, compliance checks, and regulatory requirements to clear your shipments swiftly, avoiding unnecessary delays at borders and ports.

Compliance • Documentation ↗
05

Container Fumigation

To protect your agricultural and sensitive cargo and comply with international phytosanitary standards, we provide professional container fumigation services. Our certified procedures eliminate pests and ensure your shipments meet all global import and export regulations safely and effectively.

Certified • Safe • Compliant ↗
06

Warehousing & Distribution

We offer secure, scalable warehousing facilities designed to optimize your inventory management and safeguard your cargo. Coupled with our integrated distribution services, we ensure your products are efficiently sorted, handled, and delivered to their final destinations.

Storage • Inventory • Distribution ↗
07

Supply Chain Management

Our comprehensive supply chain management solutions provide end-to-end visibility and control over your logistics operations. We analyze, design, and optimize your supply chain processes to reduce costs, enhance efficiency, and ensure a seamless, uninterrupted flow of goods from the manufacturer directly to the end consumer.

Visibility • Optimization • Control ↗
08

Tracking & Visibility

Empowering our clients with advanced, real-time tracking and complete visibility over their supply chain. We keep you proactively informed about the exact status and precise location of your shipments at every stage of the journey, ensuring peace of mind from departure to final delivery.

Real-Time • Visibility • Tracking ↗
